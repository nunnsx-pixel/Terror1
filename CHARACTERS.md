# 🧟 CHARACTERS BIBLE: FOUND FOOTAGE HORROR

## 1. Lucas (Protagonist / POV Camera)
* **Age:** 19
* **Role:** Camera operator / narrator.
* **Visuals:** Dark wavy/curly hair falling over forehead, subtle dark circles under eyes. Wears an oversized black hoodie and a thin 2mm sterling silver chain necklace.
* **Transformation State (Part 2, final shot):** Eyes completely desaturated/milky white (no pupils), same grayish pallor as Marcos, an empty, frozen, macabre smile. Dark circles under the eyes become deeper. Hoodie, hair and chain unchanged.

## 2. Clara (Secondary Main)
* **Age:** 20
* **Role:** The rational voice that breaks down.
* **Visuals:** Long straight dark hair in a high ponytail, light blue denim jacket over a white ribbed tank top, dark cargo utility pants.
* **Transformation State (Part 2, final shot):** Seen only as a blurred silhouette in the dark background: standing perfectly still, head tilted slightly, milky white eyes and the same empty smile as Marcos and Lucas.

## 3. Marcos (Secondary / Antagonist)
* **Age:** 22
* **Role:** Catalyst of the horror sequence.
* **Visuals:** Faded/shaved sides hair, black-framed optical glasses, red-and-black flannel checkered shirt over a white t-shirt.
* **Transformation State (Part 2):** Eyes completely desaturated/milky white (no pupils), grayish subsurface scattering skin tone, mechanical and unnatural movements. Head turns 180° while the body stays facing away; an empty, frozen, macabre smile; torn flannel at the shoulder; glasses left crooked on his face. Hands: cold, pale, long fingers, darkened nails.
